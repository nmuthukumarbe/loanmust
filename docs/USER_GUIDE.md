data entry and finance staff.

## 1. The two parts of the system

| Part | Address | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Public website** | `/` (the home page) | Customers. They read about our loans, call or WhatsApp us on **+91 99444 79708**, or fill the *Request a call back* form. |

**Every website enquiry becomes a lead automatically.** It appears under **Leads** with source
*Website / online*, status *New* and a follow-up date of today. Nobody has to re-type it.

> loan proposals we send them (`/p/…`). Both are covered below.

## 2. Signing in

1. Open the website and click **Staff login** (top right), or go straight to `/login`.
2. Type your **email address** and **password**. Click the eye icon to see what you typed.
3. Click **Sign in**.

Where you land depends on your role: Finance opens on **Finance**, Data entry on **Leads**,
everyone else on the **Dashboard**.

**First sign-in:** if the administrator gave you a temporary password, you must choose a new one
before you can continue.

**Change your password any time:** the key icon next to your name (bottom of the sidebar), or
**Account → Password**.

**Signing out:** the door icon next to your name. Always sign out on a shared computer.

> **Demo mode only:** when demo data is switched on, the login page shows **Choose your desk**
> tiles (Owner, Manager, Executive, Processing, Data entry, Finance). Clicking a tile fills in that
> role's demo account so you can try the system. These tiles never appear on a live system.

|---|---|---|---|

| 6 | Customer agrees → open the loan file | Executive / Processing | **Open loan file** |

## 6. Leads

A lead is anyone who has asked about a loan and is not yet a customer.

### Add a lead
1. **Leads → Add lead**.
2. Fill in **name** and **mobile** (required), plus city, loan type, amount and requirement.
3. Choose the **source**: Direct, Walk-in, Builder tie-up, LIC tie-up, Referral partner, Existing
   customer, Website, Social media or Campaign.
   - **Builder tie-up:** also pick the builder and project.
   - **Referral partner:** pick the partner, so their payout is raised automatically later.
   - **LIC tie-up:** note the branch or agent.
4. Add CIBIL score, income and existing EMIs if you know them. They improve the lead score and
   pre-fill the bank comparison.
5. **Save**.

If another open lead already has the same mobile number, the system warns you, so the same
person is not followed up twice.

### Work your follow-ups
- The **Dashboard** and **Leads** (filter *due today*) list everyone to call today. Overdue
  follow-ups are red.
- Open the lead → **Record follow-up**. Choose the channel (call, WhatsApp, visit), write what
  happened, and set the **next follow-up date**. A *New* lead becomes *Contacted* automatically.
- **WhatsApp** opens a chat with the person on your phone or WhatsApp Web. Nothing is sent until
  you press send.

### Lead score
Each lead gets a score with the reasons shown. The score uses CIBIL, income, employment, channel
and how many follow-ups have been made. Call hot leads first.

### Assign, close or convert
- **Update assignment** (manager): give the lead to an executive. Executives only see their own
  leads.
- **Close lead**: mark it *Lost* or *Not eligible*, with a reason. The reason is used in reports.
- **Convert to customer**: creates the customer with the credit details carried over. If a
  customer with that mobile already exists, it is reused, never duplicated.

## 7. Customers

A customer has a full credit profile: date of birth, employment type, monthly income, existing
EMIs, CIBIL score, co-applicant and property details.

- **Customers → open a customer** to see their profile, comparisons and loan files.
- **Edit** to correct details. **Every change is recorded in the audit log.**
- **Compare banks** / **New comparison** starts Rate Compare with this profile filled in.
- **Open loan file** starts a file for this customer.

Keep income, EMIs and CIBIL up to date. The bank comparison is only as good as these numbers.

---

## 8. Rate Compare — finding the best bank

This is the heart of the system. It checks **every scheme of every bank** against the
customer's profile.

1. **Rate Compare** (or **Compare banks** from a lead/customer, which fills the form for you).
2. Check the amount, tenure, income, EMIs, CIBIL, age, employment and property value.
3. Click **Compare all banks**.

For each scheme you see:
- **Rate** for this customer's CIBIL band (women's concession applied where the bank offers it);
- **EMI**, **total interest**, **processing fee + GST** and **total cost** of the loan;
- **eligible amount**, the lowest of what income (FOIR), property value (LTV) and the scheme
  allow. RBI's housing-loan LTV limits are applied automatically;
- **why not eligible**, in plain words, e.g. *"CIBIL 710 below cut-off of 725"*.

The **Recommended** card shows the cheapest total cost among banks that can fund the full amount,
with the reasons and the saving against other options. **It is never based on our commission.**

### Share with the customer
1. Click **Save & share proposal**.
2. Send the link on WhatsApp. The customer sees a clean page with the options and repayment
   schedule. They never see our commission.
3. **Saved proposals** shows each proposal and how many times the customer opened it.
4. When the customer agrees, open the proposal → **Open loan file**. The bank, scheme, amount and
   tenure are filled in for you.

A saved proposal is frozen: it always shows exactly what we advised that day, even after rates
change.

## 9. Banks & Rates

The panel of banks, HFCs and NBFCs we work with, and each one's schemes.

- **Add a lender:** name, type, relationship manager contacts and our **DSA payout %** (money
  roles only).
- **Add scheme:** rate band by CIBIL, women's concession, processing fee (with minimum and cap),
  maximum tenure, LTV, FOIR, minimum CIBIL, maximum age at maturity, who is eligible
  (salaried / self-employed), prepayment charges and benefits.
- **Rate confirmed today:** press this after checking rates with the bank. Schemes not confirmed
  for **30 days** are flagged in Rate Compare.

> The rate cards loaded at installation are **samples**. Confirm every scheme with the bank before
> advising a customer.

## 10. Loan files

A loan file follows one loan with one bank, from documents to disbursement.

### Open a file
**Loan Files → Open**, or **Open loan file** from a lead, customer or proposal. Choose the
lender and scheme, the amount and tenure. The file gets a code such as `LA-0012`.

### Stages

```
Collecting documents → Logged in → Credit appraisal / PD → Technical & legal
   → Sanctioned → Agreement & MODD → Disbursed
                                   (or Rejected / Withdrawn at any point)
```

Use **Move stage** to move the file forward. Every move is shown on the file's timeline. A
rejection needs a reason.

### Documents
The **document checklist** is built for this customer: KYC, income proof for salaried /
self-employed / pensioner / NRI, property papers (builder allotment, or title chain + EC +
khata), balance-transfer papers and co-applicant KYC.

- Mark each document **Received**, **Verified**, **Re-submit** or **N/A**.
- Upload a scan or a phone photo (up to 15 MB).
- **WhatsApp** sends the customer a status update listing what is still pending.

### Record the key events
| Button | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Record login** | File submitted to the bank | Saves the bank's application number; moves to *Logged in* |
| **Add to timeline** | Bank query, call, note | Keeps the history in one place |
| **Record sanction** | Sanction letter received | Saves amount, rate, tenure and validity (6 months by default). EMI is worked out |
| **Start MODD** / **Open MODD job** | Secured loan, registration needed | See section 11 |
| **Record disbursement** | Bank releases money (can be several tranches) | Moves to *Disbursed*; raises our **bank payout** and the **referral payout**. Cannot exceed the sanction |
| **Record fee** | Customer pays us a fee | Creates a printable receipt |

The **Dashboard** warns when a sanction will lapse within 15 days. **Pipeline** shows all files
as a board by stage, with counts and values.

## 11. MODD processing

For property-backed loans, the Memorandum of Deposit of Title Deeds must be registered.

1. Open the file → **Start MODD**. It also opens automatically at the *Agreement & MODD* stage.
2. **Stamp duty** is worked out from the state rate and cap set in **Settings**.
3. Book the **sub-registrar appointment**, then tell the customer with **WhatsApp**. Appointments
   for the next 7 days show on the Dashboard.
4. After registration, enter the **registration number and date**.
5. Record the **deposit of originals** with the bank, the list of documents and the
   acknowledgement.
6. **Save MODD**. The status moves on by itself as you fill in these facts.
7. Collect MODD charges and our service charge from the file with **Record fee**.

## 13. Referral partners and their payouts

Referral partners are people who send us customers: past customers, LIC agents, CAs, sub-DSAs,
builder sales staff and others.

### Enrol a partner
1. **Referral Partners → Add**.
2. Name, mobile, partner type.
3. **Payout rule** (owner/finance only): a **% of the disbursed amount** or a **flat amount per
   file**.
4. Payment details: UPI or bank account, and PAN for TDS. Account numbers are masked on screen.
5. Save. The partner gets a **personal referral link**. Send it to them with **WhatsApp**.

### How referrals arrive
- When someone fills the partner's link (`/refer/…`), a *New* lead is created and tagged to that
  partner.
- Staff can also use **Add lead for this partner** on the partner's page.

### Paying the partner
1. When a referred loan is disbursed, a payout is **raised automatically** under **Referral
   Payouts**, using the partner's rule with TDS deducted.
2. A **Manager / Owner** opens it and clicks **Approve payout**. They can also hold or cancel it,
   with a reason.
3. **Finance** pays it and clicks **Mark paid**, entering the payment mode and **UTR**. Only
   approved payouts can be paid. Paid payouts cannot be changed.
4. Send the partner a thank-you with the payment details on WhatsApp.

## 14. Bank payouts (our commission)

Every disbursement tranche creates a **payout receivable** from the bank, at the payout % on the
file.

Under **Bank Payouts** (finance):
1. **Save invoice** when you bill the bank.
2. **Record receipt** when money arrives. Part payments add up, and any difference over ₹1 is
   flagged.
3. Payouts unpaid **60 days** after disbursement are marked **overdue** automatically every
   morning.
4. **Write off** an amount that won't be recovered (a reason is required and recorded).

## 17. Administration (owner / admin)

### Users
**Users → Add user**: name, email, role and a temporary password. The person must change it at
first sign-in. To stop someone's access, click **Disable** next to them in the user list. Don't share one login between
two people, or the audit log can't tell who did what.

### Roles & Permissions
A table of every role against every screen, with **view / add / edit / delete / export**
ticks. Use **Tick all** / **Untick all** for a whole row, then save. Changes apply at the
user's next page load.

### Audit log
Every change is recorded: who, when, and the old and new values. Open **Audit Log** to search,
or see the panel on each record. **Export** gives the owner or an auditor an Excel copy.

### Settings
Agency name and trade name, owner, phone, email, address, DSA code, GSTIN, PAN, code prefixes,
MODD stamp duty % and cap, MODD service charge, processing-fee GST %, proposal validity, default
referral % and TDS %, and the accent colour. Click **Save settings**.

> **Tip:** the agency name shown inside the staff desk comes from **Settings**. Set the name to
> *Sri Sai Associates* and the phone to *+91 99444 79708* there.

## 18. Everyday tips

- **Start the day on the Dashboard:** clear the follow-ups, check sanctions expiring and MODD
  appointments.
- **Record things as they happen:** a follow-up, a bank query, a document received. The timeline
  then tells the full story to anyone who picks up the file.
- **Always pick the correct lead source and referral partner.** Payouts and channel reports
  depend on it.
- **Confirm bank rates** at least monthly (*Rate confirmed today*).
- **WhatsApp buttons never send anything on their own.** You always see and send the message
  yourself.
- **Use your own login** and sign out on shared computers.

## 19. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"Those details do not match an active account"* | Check the email spelling and Caps Lock. Ask the admin to reset your password or re-enable your user. |
| A menu or button is missing | Your role doesn't have that permission. Ask the owner to check **Roles & Permissions**. |
| Executive can't see a lead or file | Executives only see their own work. A manager can re-assign it with **Update assignment**. |
| Sent to the *No access* page | Same as above: the screen isn't allowed for your role. |
| Duplicate-mobile warning on a new lead | Search the mobile number in **Leads**. Continue the existing lead instead of creating another. |
| A bank shows *Not eligible* | Read the reason in the comparison. It names the rule (CIBIL, FOIR, LTV, age) that failed. Correct the profile if a number was wrong. |
| Disbursement refused | The amount is more than the sanction left. Check the sanction amount and previous tranches. |
| A referral payout didn't appear | Check that the lead and file were tagged to the partner, and that a disbursement was recorded. |
| Upload fails | Files must be under 15 MB. Take the photo at a lower resolution or upload a PDF. |
| Website enquiries not showing | Look under **Leads** with source *Website / online* and status *New*. |

For anything else, contact the office administrator.
