Agency profile

Code prefixes

New records are numbered with these prefixes, e.g. LD-0001. Existing codes are not changed.

Referral programme

Used when a partner has no payout rule of their own, and for TDS on every referral payout.

MODD charges

Defaults for estimating Memorandum of Deposit of Title Deeds charges to the customer.

Stamp duty on MODD is state-specific and changes; confirm the current rate for your state.

Rate compare & proposals

Used when comparing banks and generating proposals for a customer.

Workspace accent

The colour used for buttons, highlights and the sidebar for everyone in your agency.
