What each role can do

Owner / Admin — everything, including settings, users and the audit log.

Processing — documents, bank login, sanction, MODD and disbursement tracking.

Data Entry — captures leads, customers and documents; no money screens.

Finance — bank payouts, referral payouts, fee receipts.
