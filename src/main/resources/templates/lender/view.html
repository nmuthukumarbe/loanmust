How the advisor uses this

  • At or above the best-rate CIBIL the customer is quoted the lowest rate; at the minimum CIBIL, the top of the range; in between, a banded rate.
  • Below the minimum CIBIL the scheme is shown as not eligible, with the reason.
  • FOIR (income) and LTV (property value) cap the loan the lender would sanction.
  • Schemes are ranked by total cost to the customer, never by our payout. Stale rates are flagged.
