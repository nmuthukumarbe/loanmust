How these numbers are used

CIBIL banding. A customer at or above the best-rate CIBIL is quoted rate from. At the minimum CIBIL they get rate to, and in between the advisor interpolates. Below the minimum the scheme is shown as not eligible.

FOIR (fixed obligations to income) is the share of monthly income that all EMIs, old and new, may take. It sets the largest EMI, and so the largest loan, the lender will allow.

LTV (loan to value) caps the loan as a percentage of the property value. RBI limits still apply on top.

The eligible loan is the lowest of the FOIR limit, the LTV limit and the maximum loan amount.
