MODD registrations

Mortgage deposit jobs for secured loans, from originals collected to deposit with the bank.

What is MODD? The Memorandum of Deposit of Title Deeds creates the equitable mortgage for a home loan or LAP. It is registered at the sub-registrar office (stamp duty and registration fee apply), and the customer's original title documents are then deposited with the bank. Most lenders need it before or alongside disbursement. A job is started from the loan file's Start MODD button.
